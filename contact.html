FAQ

Will it fit my wrist?

The 19 cm length fits 16.5–19.5 cm wrists comfortably. Outside that range, message us and we can adjust links.

Is it shower-proof?

Splash-proof, yes. Rain and handwash are nothing to it. Please take it off for swimming, especially pools and the sea.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

Place the order, we call to confirm, the courier collects the box total on delivery. Anywhere in Bangladesh.

Exchange policy?

7 days, unworn, box and card intact. We cover Dhaka courier pickups.
